## Operating points and Figure 1

The numerator and denominator for each detector are in the corresponding `T*_operating_point_*.json`. Equation 1 uses prior 0.01. The adapted `tools/confidence_intervals.py` recomputes Wilson endpoint ranges from the included A2, A3, and overwrite A4 operating points. `common/anchors.py` implements refusal at LR+ ≤ 1 and the conservative rule-of-three case when no false positives are observed. A1 has no measured operating point. A3 mixes a path-level TPR with an event-level FPR. The manuscript's per-logon 5/352 result is checked from the two window summaries, which have three and two detected events respectively. Figure 1's A2/A3/A4 labels 0.82/0.91/0.46 come from Table II. The chain itself did not emit A3.

The comparison figures in Table II are external benchmarks except the Sigma baseline below. T1593's 272/31,898, T1190's F1 0.974 on approximately 152,000 files, T1021's 94.5% and under nine alerts/day on 780M logins, and T1486's zero misses with at most 5% false alarms have different inputs and must be checked against the cited papers.
