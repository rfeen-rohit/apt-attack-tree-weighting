# Data, terms, and preprocessing

Publisher URLs and terms below come from the project draft and local documentation. This offline review did not verify current access, terms, source versions, or download checksums. The candidate contains no third-party data or vendored code. Saved statistics are project-generated excerpts. Confirm the authors’ authority and the underlying data-use terms before distribution.

| Study | Source and terms to confirm | Acquisition and preprocessing |
|---|---|---|
| Selection | MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise v19.2 STIX bundle and ATT&CK terms. v18 is the leaf-mapping vocabulary. | Obtain the versioned Enterprise STIX JSON from [MITRE ATT&CK STIX data](https://github.com/mitre-attack/attack-stix-data). Supply the local bundle to `selection/attestation.py`. The cited vendor weights and v18 leaf mapping require the underlying references and manual adjudication. |
| Literature | OpenAlex and dblp. Check terms for any future redistribution of retrieved metadata and abstracts. | Preserve date range, title/abstract scope, eligibility criteria and deduplication. | T1190 | WordPress, Joomla and Drupal core. Check each source revision's GPL notices. Exact source revision pins and the archived manifest-to-run linkage remain unverified. |

Keep baseline event files, raw logs and record-level outputs local. |
